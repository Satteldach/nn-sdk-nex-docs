enum nn::nex::Ranking2ResetMode::Ranking2ResetMode

The periodic reset mode used when resetting rankings.

Enumerators
NOTHING 

No periodic resets.

EVERYDAY 

Every day, at a specified time.

EVERYWEEK 

Every week at the specified time of day, on the specified day of the week.

MULTI_MONTH 

At the specified time of day, on the specified day, of the specified months.

MULTI_MONTH_WEEKDAY 

At the specified time of day, on the first specified weekday, of the specified months.
